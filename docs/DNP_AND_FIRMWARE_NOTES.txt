AIRGUARD Rev C — Do Not Populate (DNP)
======================================

R7 — 4.7k, 0603
Reason: existing PCB connection is GPIO1-to-GND. Leaving R7 unpopulated removes the fixed pull-down.
Firmware: configure GPIO1 as INPUT_PULLUP. SW1 remains active-low (pressed = LOW).

U3 — CMT-8540S-SMT-TR
Reason: magnetic transducer is externally driven and its rated test condition draws up to 150mA at 5V, 4kHz, 50% duty. Existing PCB connects it directly between BUZZER_DRV and GND, without a transistor driver.
Firmware: disable buzzer output for this revision. Use OLED messages/indicators instead.

Do not hand-fit either part on this revision. The buzzer requires a separate low-side driver design and a new routed/verified PCB revision.
